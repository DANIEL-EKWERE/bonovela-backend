Understanding the word count: The 150,000-word requirement means the completed manuscript must contain at least 150,000 words in total. It is the book's overall word count—not 150,000 words per month. The $60 profit-share requirement is a separate earnings condition. Reaching the word count alone does not qualify a book for the bonus.

Questions & Answers

1. Does Bonovela currently offer Exclusive Contracts?

No. Exclusive benefits are marked as coming soon. The current contract benefit described here is for Non-Exclusive authors.

2. How is the first Short Stories bonus calculated?

The author must accumulate $100 in royalty share within the 136-day qualifying period. After verification, the author can claim a $50 bonus.

3. Does the second Short Stories bonus have a deadline?

No. After claiming the first $50 reward, the author can continue toward an additional $500 in royalty share without another time limit.

4. Does 150,000 words guarantee the Non-Exclusive Completion Bonus?

No. The book must also be published on Bonovela first, reach at least $60 in total author profit share, and be marked completed within 136 days after signing.

5. What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The minimum withdrawal threshold is $100 USD. A request can be made when the eligible balance reaches this amount, in line with Bonovela's payment procedures.
