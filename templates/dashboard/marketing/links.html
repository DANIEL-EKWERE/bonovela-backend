Book-first reader journey

Advertisement
Reader sees an ad featuring a specific book cover.
Ad click
The link carries the campaign and the Book ID.
Book preview page
A mobile web page for that exact book — cover, synopsis, Chapter 1 or Chapters 1–5.
App download
Google Play on Android (Book ID kept in the install referrer), App Store on iOS.
Install & first open
The app reads the referrer and remembers the book.
Login or registration
The pending Book ID is kept through sign-in.
Direct book opening
Reader lands on the advertised book.
Attribution
Clicks, previews, store visits, installs, registrations and purchases are recorded against the link.
